The Huang File is an independent editorial concept focused on Jensen Huang, NVIDIA, and the people and institutions around the AI buildout.

01
OUR METHOD

The story is the record.

We report what has been confirmed by primary documents or direct statements. We attribute allegations to the outlet making them. We separate personal conduct from corporate governance without pretending the two can never affect one another.

When an allegation cannot be independently verified, the file says so. When a person has not been charged or publicly identified through an official process, we consider whether naming them serves a demonstrable public interest.

01

Primary sources first

Board statements, filings, direct remarks, and court records outrank summaries and social posts.

02

Clear labels

Confirmed, alleged, disputed, and unknown are distinct categories—not tones of voice.

03

Proportionate privacy

Public status does not erase all personal privacy. We avoid salacious detail that does not explain a matter of public interest.

04

Corrections over certainty

Developing stories change. Corrections and meaningful updates belong in the reporting, not hidden at the bottom.
