THE LONG VIEW

Three decades of conviction

NVIDIA’s rise was not one AI moment. It was a sequence of expensive, unpopular bets that only looked inevitable after they paid off.

1993
ORIGIN

A company sketched at Denny’s

Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem found NVIDIA with the goal of building better graphics processing hardware. Huang becomes president and CEO.

1999
THE GPU

GeForce 256 raises the stakes

NVIDIA releases a processor marketed as the world’s first GPU, sharpening its identity around parallel computing and high-performance graphics.

2006
THE LONG BET

CUDA opens the GPU

NVIDIA invests in a software platform that lets developers use its chips for general-purpose computing. The bet precedes modern generative AI by well over a decade.

“The software is the thing.” — the bet behind CUDA
2016
AI INFRASTRUCTURE

The data-center turn

NVIDIA positions its GPUs around deep learning, accelerating a shift from gaming hardware toward the infrastructure of modern artificial intelligence.

2022
THE BOOM

Generative AI goes mainstream

Demand for accelerated computing surges as large language models move from research labs into commercial products. Huang becomes the public face of the boom.

Investigation and a personal admission
